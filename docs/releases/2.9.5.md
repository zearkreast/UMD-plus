- 📶 **Telefonda yalnızca Wi-Fi ile indir.** Ayarlar → İndirmeler'deki yeni anahtar açıkken, mobil verideyken indirmeler başlamaz ve "Wi-Fi bekleniyor" der; Wi-Fi gelince kendiliğinden başlar. Süren bir indirme Wi-Fi kesilse de devam eder.

- 🔎 **Geçmişte süzgeç.** Geçmişi artık türe (video, ses, fotoğraf) ve siteye göre daraltabilir, en yeniden ya da en eskiden sıralayabilirsin. Arama bunlarla birlikte çalışır.

- ⚡ **Tek tıkla indir.** Ayarlar → Genel'deki yeni anahtar açıkken, tarayıcı uzantısından gönderdiğin her video ana sayfada açılmadan doğrudan kuyruğa girer. Art arda birkaç video gönderince artık yalnızca sonuncusu kalmıyor, hepsi iniyor. Telefonda aynısı başka uygulamadan paylaşılan bağlantı için geçerli.
- 🕘 **Ana sayfada son indirilenler.** Bağlantı alanı boşken son üç indirme altında görünür; tıklayınca dosya açılır.
- ⚖️ **Lisans GPL-3.0 oldu.** UMD+ bu sürümden itibaren GNU Genel Kamu Lisansı 3 ile yayınlanıyor. Üzerine kurulduğu unsalable/downloader projesinin MIT bildirimi `LICENSE-MIT` dosyasında duruyor.

- 📡 **İnternet kesilince indirmeler bekliyor.** Hat düşerse indirme "Başarısız" olmuyor; "İnternet bekleniyor" diyerek yerinde bekliyor ve hat gelince kaldığı yerden sürüyor. Önce 5, 15 ve 30 saniye sonra, ardından dakikada bir yeniden deniyor; "Şimdi dene" beklemeyi kısaltır. Kuyruk bu sırada bitmiş sayılmadığı için "Bitince: bilgisayarı kapat" de hiçbir şey inmeden çalışmıyor. Önceden birkaç saniyelik bir kesinti bütün kuyruğu başarısız yapabiliyordu.
- ▶️ **Açılışta kuyruğu sürdür.** Ayarlar → Genel'deki yeni anahtar açıkken, uygulama yeniden açıldığında yarım kalan ve bekleyen indirmeler kendiliğinden devam eder. Kapalıyken eskisi gibi duraklatılmış gelirler.
- 🐢 **Hız sınırı artık tam kullanılıyor.** Sınır açıkken tek başına inen bir yayın (HLS, DASH) sınırın tamamını kullanır. Önceden sınır, ayardaki eşzamanlı indirme sayısına bölünüyordu: 3 MB/s sınırla tek bir video 1 MB/s ile iniyordu.

- 📁 **Dosya adları düzeldi.** Şablona `{title}.{ext}` yazınca uzantı artık iki kez eklenmiyor. Japonca, Kiril ve benzeri yazılardaki uzun başlıklar da Latin başlıklar kadar uzun kalıyor; önceden 30–45 karaktere kadar kısalabiliyordu.

## English

- **Download on Wi-Fi only, on the phone.** With the new switch in Settings → Downloads on, downloads do not start on mobile data and say they are waiting for Wi-Fi; they start by themselves once it is back. One already running carries on if Wi-Fi drops.

- **A size limit in Convert.** A video can be converted to "no more than 8, 10, 25, 50, 100 or 500 MB". The bitrate, and the frame if need be, are set so the file stays under that size, for the upload limits of messaging apps.
- **Folders from the template.** A slash in the file name template makes folders: `{platform}/{creator}/{title}` sorts downloads by site and channel. A slash used to be turned into a dash.
- **Keep the cover as a picture.** With the new switch in Settings → Downloads on, the cover of a video or a song is saved beside the file, under the same name.
- **Filters in the history.** The history can be narrowed to a kind (video, audio, photo) and a site, and listed newest or oldest first. The search works together with them.
- **A calmer Connection page.** Which browser is connected and which sites are signed in now show at a glance. The long explanations are still there, under three headings that open on a press.
- **A speed chart in the detail window.** A download's details now draw its speed over the last minute, so it shows at a glance whether a slow download is slow throughout or fast with stops in it. Stretches in which nothing arrived are shaded.
- **Paste several links at once.** More than one link on the clipboard, or in a dropped text, shows on Home as a list: take out what you do not want and queue the rest with one button. Each downloads with the default quality and format.
- **One-click download.** With the new switch in Settings → General on, every video sent from the browser extension goes straight into the queue without opening on Home. Sending several in a row now downloads all of them, not only the last. On the phone the same goes for a link shared from another app.
- **Recent downloads on Home.** While the link field is empty, the last three downloads show under it; a press opens the file.
- **The licence is now GPL-3.0.** From this version UMD+ is released under the GNU General Public License, version 3. The MIT notice of unsalable/downloader, which it is built on, is kept in `LICENSE-MIT`.
- **Downloads come in three parts.** The list is divided into Active, Waiting and Finished, so a paused download no longer sits lost among finished ones. Finished shows its latest five, and the rest on a press.
- **The queue's order shows, and can be changed.** Waiting downloads stand in the order they will start, numbered. Drag a row by its handle to move it; "Download next" now visibly moves the row to the top.
- **An update installs whatever the repository is called.** The app takes the update file under either name the repository has had. A copy used to refuse every update once the repository was renamed.
- **Downloads wait out a dropped connection.** When the line goes down a download no longer fails: it says it is waiting for the internet, stays where it is and carries on from where it stopped once the line is back. It tries again after 5, 15 and 30 seconds, then once a minute; Try now cuts the wait short. The queue does not count as finished meanwhile, so a shutdown asked for after it does not happen with nothing downloaded. A break of a few seconds used to be able to fail the whole queue.
- **Carry on with the queue at launch.** With the new switch in Settings → General on, downloads that were running or waiting continue by themselves when the app opens. Off, they come back paused as before.
- **A speed limit is used in full.** With a limit set, a stream (HLS, DASH) downloading on its own gets all of it. The limit used to be divided by the number of downloads allowed at once: under a 3 MB/s limit a single video ran at 1 MB/s.
- **Paste finds the link in a text.** A link among other words is picked out, and of two links on two lines the first is taken; they used to be joined into one broken address.
- **File names are right.** `{title}.{ext}` in the template no longer adds the extension twice, and a long title in Japanese, Cyrillic and similar scripts keeps as much as a Latin one, where it could be cut to 30–45 characters.
- **No English labels in another language.** "Best - MP4", "Auto", "Original" and "Audio" are written in the interface's language.
- **No 100% before the end.** A download's row rounded its percentage up, and read 100% while the last of a large file was still coming.
- **The editor reads time by the clock.** An hour of 29.97 or 23.976 fps video reads 1:00:00, not 59:56.
- **Pages no longer shift sideways** when a scrollbar appears.
- **Download speed reads steadily.** The speed shown is now the average of the last few seconds, so a line pausing for a second or two no longer drops it to zero each time, while a lasting change still shows within seconds. Connections no longer stop while a download saves its progress to disk, which on a hard disk or an external drive meant a short break every two seconds. A paused and resumed stream (HLS, DASH) no longer shows an absurd speed for its first seconds.
- **Automatic updates can be turned off.** With "Update automatically" off in Settings → General, the app no longer looks for, downloads or installs a new version by itself. "Check for updates" on the About page shows an Update button when it finds one, and the install happens only after that press, once nothing is running. On the phone, no update is offered at launch.
- **Picking from TikTok sends the video.** Clicking a video in For You, Following and the other feeds now sends that video. It used to say the item could not be sent, and clicking the blurred edge beside the video sent its cover picture instead.
- **The end of a download shows.** Merging picture and sound and writing the details into the file now show their own name and percentage, rather than sitting at 97% as if stuck.
- **Quit when the queue runs out quits.** A waiting update no longer restarts the app in its place; it installs at the next launch. Nor does an installer start while the computer is about to shut down.
- **The extension reads more carefully.** Codes such as `&amp;lt;` in a stream's manifest are decoded once, not twice, and a page title loses its site's name in a safer way. GitHub's code scanning alerts are cleared in this version.
- **The detail window tells the truth** for a stream in pieces (HLS, DASH): how many pieces download at once, rather than "One connection". They always did; the window did not show it.
- **Sending a page sends its video.** Right-click → send page, or `Alt+Shift+S`, now sends the video the page is playing when there is one, rather than the page address, which a site with an embedded player cannot be downloaded from.
- **Playlists and channels** open in seconds, however long. Pick the videos you want; they land in a folder named after the list.
- **Subtitles** in the language you choose, beside the video or inside it.
- **Details written into the file:** title, creator, date, chapters and a cover.
- **A speed limit**, and **scheduled hours** for the queue. When it runs out the computer can sleep or shut down, after a 60-second countdown you can call off.
- **Right-click in the extension** to send a link, a video or a page; `Alt+Shift+S` sends the page.
- **A link you downloaded before** is pointed out.
- **A new interface**, and the first **Android** and **Arch Linux** builds.
